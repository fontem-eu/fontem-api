The knowledge graph (Cypher)

Neo4j, queried with Cypher. This is the store for entities and the relationships between them: who was awarded what, who owns whom, who lobbied for which act.

Node labels

The ones an analysis normally starts from:

  • Company, Authority, Lobbyist, SanctionedEntity — the parties.
  • Contract, Notice, Cpv — public procurement and its sector codes.
  • CohesionProject, Programme, Fund — EU cohesion funding.
  • InvestmentFund, Listing, FinancialYear — corporate and market data.
  • NUTSRegion, Nuts — geography.
  • LegalAct, Petition, Disclosure — the legislative record.

Labels such as BackfillCheckpoint, ConsolidationRun, MergeEvent and TedWatermark are pipeline bookkeeping. They are not analysis data and should not appear in a chart.

Relationship types

AWARDED, AWARDED_TO, BID_ON, SUBSIDIARY_OF, SAME_AS, SANCTIONED, UNDER_PROGRAMME, FINANCED_BY, LOCATED_IN, PART_OF, CHILD_OF, MODIFIES, NOTICE_OF, LISTED_AS, REGISTERED_BY, FILED_BY, REPORTED, ANSWERED_BY, PRODUCED, APPLIED.

Two things to know before counting

The graph holds duplicates. The same company can appear as several nodes — country subsidiaries, spelling variants, punctuation. SAME_AS links some of them, and it is reviewed: an unreviewed link is a candidate, not a fact. A count(DISTINCT c) over name matches will overcount.

Direction is not always what it reads like. Match relationships without a direction arrow unless you have checked which way round they are stored.

MATCH (c:Company)-[:AWARDED_TO]-(ct:Contract)
RETURN c.name AS company, count(ct) AS contracts
ORDER BY contracts DESC LIMIT 20
