Creating a Data Studio project

A project is created empty and named. The name is the only required field, and it is worth spending a moment on: it is what appears in the Studio list and in any investigation the project is attached to.

Attaching to an investigation

A project can be attached to an investigation at creation or later. An investigation groups the work behind one line of enquiry — several articles can be written from a single investigation, and its projects are the evidence they draw on. Attaching is what makes a chart reusable across those articles instead of stranded in one.

Sharing

Projects are private to their owner until shared. Access is granted per user, at one of two levels:

  • viewer — can open the project, read its queries and see its plots.
  • editor — can add and change sources and plots.

Access granted through an investigation is separate from access granted directly on the project; the effective level is the stronger of the two.

Renaming and deleting

Renaming affects only the label. Deleting a project removes its sources and plots with it, and is not reversible — a plot inserted into a published story keeps its rendered snapshot, but the project behind it is gone.
